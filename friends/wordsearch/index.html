    How to play

    Eleven clues, eleven answers in the grid. Work out each answer, then find it: across, down or diagonally, forwards or backwards. Drag from the first letter to the last. The number in brackets is how many letters.

    Episode boards give you a title with a word missing — “The One with the _____” — and the missing word is the one to find.

    Ninety minutes, in ten. The clock runs ninety minutes while ten real minutes pass, and your score falls as it goes. A wrong drag adds a minute or more.

    A twelfth word is hidden too. The secret bonus has its own clue and is worth ten.

    114 is the ceiling, the eleven and the bonus together. Help cards are for other boards only, not today’s.
