How to play

  1. Eleven clues about Friends, crossing in one grid.
  2. Tap a square and type. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
  3. Every clue is scored out of 100 for the board — finish it and you keep the day.
  4. A new crossword every day. Play every day to build a streak.
