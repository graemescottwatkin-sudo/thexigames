HOW TO PLAY One door, one player
  1. Pick one of the eleven clubs. One door a day — choose carefully, because the other ten stay shut.
  2. You get one spell of that player’s career free: the club, the years, the games and the goals.
  3. Stuck? Two substitutions. The first shows his whole career, the second his nationality and age. Both cost points.
  4. Name him. The clock runs to ninety and the board falls from 114, so the sooner the better.
