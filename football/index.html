About The XI Games

The daily football puzzle club. Ten different ways to test your football knowledge, with eleven answers in each game and a fresh set of puzzles every day, the same for everyone.

More about how it works

Every game in the family follows the same rules of the competition: eleven answers a day, scored out of 114 — the points available across a 38-match season — with wins, draws and losses that build a league table of your own season. Finish two games in a day and it is a win; one is a draw.

Crossword XI wears the number one shirt: a daily football crossword with eleven clues drawn from the history of the game — title winners, transfers, grounds, nicknames and the players everyone half-remembers. Every game keeps its earlier boards at their own addresses, in previous dailies.

No account is needed to play. Signing in keeps your results and your season on every device you use.
